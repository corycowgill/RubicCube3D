How to Play

  • Rotate camera: Drag empty space (or two-finger drag on mobile).
  • Rotate a slice: Drag across a sticker on the cube in the direction you want it to spin.
  • Zoom: Mouse wheel, pinch on mobile, the +/− buttons, or the +/− keys.
  • Shuffle: Tap the Shuffle button to scramble.
  • Undo: Reverses your last move (only counts after shuffling).
  • Goal: Make every face one solid color.
  • Tutorial: Tap the 🎓 button (or press T) for a step-by-step algorithm guide.

The timer starts as soon as you make a move on a shuffled cube.
